Privacy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Who is responsible

PrayerAid is run by Oussama Ouabyt, Strassen, Luxembourg. Contact: oussama.ouabyt@gmail.com

Your location

If you allow it, your phone gives the app your position so it can show nearby places. Your position stays on your phone: PrayerAid does not send it to us or store it on a server. Your browser loads map images from OpenStreetMap, and when you use “Use my location” in the add-place form, the pin position is sent to OpenStreetMap’s address service (Nominatim) to fill in the address. Both see your IP address and the map area, like any website you visit.

Stored on your phone only

Your language, your prayer-space preference (men / women / all), your saved places and your last map position are kept in your browser’s storage. They never leave your phone. Clearing your browser data removes them.

When you suggest a place

The form sends the place details, photos, and your name, email and (optional) phone number. They are delivered by email through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co, United States) to our Gmail inbox (Google). We use them only to check the place and contact you about it. Legal basis: your consent when you send the form (GDPR art. 6(1)(a)). We delete your contact details within 12 months after a decision on your submission. Place details and photos that we publish stay on the map. Transfers to the US rely on the providers’ standard contractual clauses.

Visit statistics

We count visits and anonymous actions (for example “someone confirmed they prayed at this place”) with GoatCounter. It uses no cookies, does not track you across sites and does not store personal data.

What we don’t do

No ads, no advertising partners, no selling of data, no tracking cookies, no location history.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the data you sent us, or withdraw your consent, by writing to the address above. You can also complain to the Luxembourg data protection authority, CNPD (cnpd.public.lu).
